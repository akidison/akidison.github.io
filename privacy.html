Data Collection

Our apps are designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What we DON'T collect:

  • Personal identification information
  • Location data
  • Contact information
  • Usage tracking or analytics
  • Device identifiers for advertising

Local Storage

Some of our apps may store data locally on your device to save your progress, preferences, or created content. This data:

  • Stays on your device only
  • Is never transmitted to our servers
  • Can be deleted by uninstalling the app

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store or by Google through Google Play. We do not have access to your payment information, Apple ID, Google account, or purchase history beyond what the store provides for transaction verification.

Advertising

Some of our apps may display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob). These services may collect anonymous data according to their own privacy policies. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. All data processing (if any) occurs locally on the device.

Waves — Dark Mesh Chat

Waves is a peer-to-peer mesh messenger that works without internet using Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct. In addition to the general policies above, the following applies specifically to Waves:

  • Mesh Communication: All messages are transmitted directly between devices via Apple's MultipeerConnectivity. No data passes through our servers.
  • End-to-End Encryption: Private messages are encrypted using Curve25519 key exchange and ChaChaPoly. Only sender and recipient can read them. Relay nodes see only encrypted data.
  • Location Groups: When you create a location-based group, we store only the group name, GPS coordinates, radius, and password hash (if set) in Firebase Firestore. No user names, messages, or device identifiers are stored.
  • Disappearing Messages: Messages auto-delete based on your TTL setting (15 minutes to 2 hours).
  • No Accounts: Waves requires no registration, no email, no phone number.
  • Permissions: Bluetooth and Local Network are required for mesh. Microphone (voice messages), Location (location groups), Photos (photo messages), and Nearby Interaction (AR Radar) are optional and requested only when used.

For the full Waves privacy policy, visit Waves Privacy Policy.

Srpski — Serbian Words

Srpski is a Serbian vocabulary app for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Android. In addition to the general policies above, the following applies specifically to Srpski:

  • No Accounts: Srpski requires no registration, email or phone number.
  • Your Progress: Learning progress, word sets, associations and settings are stored on your device. On Apple devices, progress, word sets, associations and records sync between your own devices through your iCloud account — Apple’s storage, which we cannot access. On Android, this data stays on the device and may be included in your device backup in your Google account, depending on your Android backup settings; we cannot access it.
  • Microphone, Camera and Photos: Used only when you choose to record how a word sounds, take a photo for a word card, or pick or paste a picture. Recordings, photos and pictures stay on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Notifications: If you allow notifications, Srpski registers your device with Pushwoosh, our notification delivery service, to send review reminders and app news. It sends the notification token, an app installation identifier (not the advertising identifier), device model, operating system version (iOS or Android), language, time zone, app version, approximate location (city and country, derived from the IP address) and in-app events such as app and screen opens. This data is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. You can turn notifications off in the app’s Settings or in your phone’s settings.
  • Analytics and Crashes: Srpski uses Google Firebase Analytics for anonymous usage statistics and Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports. This data is not linked to your identity and is not used for advertising or tracking; the advertising identifier is not collected.
  • Purchases: Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your payment details; the app only sees your subscription status.
  • Widgets, Lock Screen and Apple Watch: They show words from your progress; this data moves only between your own devices. On Android, widgets and the Lock Screen word card are drawn on the device and send no data.
