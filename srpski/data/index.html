Source

This data is derived from English Wiktionary (Serbo-Croatian entries), using the machine-readable extract published by kaikki.org (October 2026 dump). Wiktionary text is written by Wiktionary contributors and licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license.

Changes we made

  • Kept only the words used in Srpski and only Serbian (ekavian) forms.
  • Removed stress and length marks from inflected forms; the headword keeps its stress mark.
  • Turned etymology templates into a short source line: language, word in Latin script and, for loanwords, what the source word means.
  • Added our own short meanings for about 120 source words of loanwords.

Format

A JSON object keyed by the Serbian word in Latin script. Every field is optional.

acHeadword with stress mark, e.g. kȕća
cyCyrillic spelling
nNoun declension: singular and plural, 7 cases each (nom, gen, dat, acc, voc, instr, loc)
fuFuture tense without a pronoun, 6 persons
imImperative: ti, mi, vi
aoAorist, 6 persons
vnVerbal noun
dim, aug, radjDiminutive, augmentative, relational adjective
der, relDerived and related words
syn, antSynonyms and antonyms
ijIjekavian variant
etOrigin: src [language, word], g meaning of the source word (ru, en), k “c” for a calque, ru and en related words
